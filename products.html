Order Terms

Minimum Order & Supply Terms

Built for wholesale importers, distributors, and hypermarket procurement teams.

01

Minimum Order (MOQ)

One full 40ft refrigerated container (reefer) per commodity. LCL is not offered for fresh produce — cold chain integrity requires FCL loads.

02

Origin Regions

Onions: Nashik, Maharashtra · Potatoes: Gujarat & Punjab · Garlic: Madhya Pradesh · Tomatoes: Karnataka & Maharashtra · Spices: Rajasthan, Gujarat & MP · Rice: Punjab, Haryana & Gujarat.

03

Payment Terms

30% T/T deposit on order confirmation; 70% against shipping document copies. Irrevocable L/C at sight for approved buyers.

04

Lead Time

7–14 days from order confirmation to vessel departure, subject to harvest window and port slot availability.

05

Incoterms

CIF and CFR to GCC ports (Jebel Ali, Jeddah, Dammam, etc.). FOB Nhava Sheva or Mundra available on request.

06

Quality Guarantee

Pre-loading inspection reports (SGS / Bureau Veritas) available. Claims protocol documented on our FAQ page.

Premium Basmati Rice

Preferred by fine-dining establishments and bulk distributors across Saudi Arabia and the UAE, our Basmati rice provides exceptional grain length, exquisite aroma, and a non-sticky texture post-cooking.

  • GCC Varieties: 1121 Basmati (Steam, Sella, Golden Sella), Traditional Basmati, and Pusa Basmati.
  • Quality Specifications: Extra-long grain elongation, minimum moisture content, and perfectly aged for authentic flavor.
  • Packaging: 10kg, 20kg, 25kg, and 50kg multi-layer PP, Jute, or custom non-woven bags with Bilingual (Arabic/English) typography.

Premium Non-Basmati Rice

Engineered for institutional catering, airline kitchens, and high-volume food service across Kuwait, Oman, and Qatar where consistency and crop yield are critical.

  • GCC Varieties: Sona Masoori, PR11, IR64, Parboiled Rice, and broken/white rice options.
  • Compliance: Low moisture thresholds, strictly monitored to withstand regional warehouse climates.
  • Packaging: Heavy-duty 25kg and 50kg export packaging.

High Quality Pulses

A vital protein staple for regional packaging units and food manufacturers. Our pulses undergo multi-stage machine sorting and laser-grading to ensure zero impurities.

  • GCC Varieties: Kabuli Chana (Chickpeas), Masoor Dal (Red Lentils), Toor Dal, Moong Dal, and Urad Dal.
  • Quality Profile: Zero chemical additives, high protein retention, and moisture-controlled packaging to guarantee shelf-life continuity.
  • Packaging: Standard 25kg / 50kg bulk export sacs.

Authentic Indian Spices

Unadulterated spices carrying rich natural essential oils to deliver deep color and intense flavor profiles required by Middle Eastern culinary manufacturing.

  • GCC Varieties: Whole Red Chilli, Turmeric Fingers, Cumin Seeds, Coriander Seeds, Black Pepper, and Green Cardamom.
  • Safety & Testing: Strictly tested for Aflatoxin and pesticide residues to clear strict GSO and SFDA border inspections.
  • Packaging: Multi-layer moisture-barrier bulk packaging (25/50kg).
