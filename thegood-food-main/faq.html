Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for wholesale importers and procurement desks sourcing Fresh Vegetables, Rice, Pulses, and Spices from India to the GCC.

01

Logistics & Containers

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) and container specification?

Our standard MOQ is based on full container loads (FCL): 40ft High-Cube Reefers (~20–29 MT) for fresh produce, and 20ft Normal Dry Containers (~24–26 MT for rice & pulses, ~17–20 MT for spices) to ensure unbroken logistics and quality integrity.

Which GCC ports do you service with direct sea freight?

We provide regular, weekly container sailings from Nhava Sheva (JNPT) and Mundra Port to Jebel Ali & Khalifa Port (UAE), Dammam & Jeddah Islamic Port (Saudi Arabia), Shuwaikh (Kuwait), Hamad Port (Qatar), and Sohar/Salalah (Oman) with transit times of 3 to 7 days.

How do you prevent spoilage and moisture damage during transit?

For fresh produce, we utilize micro-climate pre-cooling and 24/7 telematics-controlled CA reefers. For dry commodities (rice, pulses, spices), we use LDPE liners, container-grade silica gel desiccants, and sealed kraft paper linings to completely prevent moisture sweating and condensation.

02

Quality, Certifications & Compliance

What official registrations and export certificates do you hold?

We are a certified exporter registered with APEDA (RCMC) for fresh agricultural produce & rice, and the Spices Board of India (CRES) for authentic Indian spices. We operate with central FSSAI licensing and ISO 22000 / HACCP food safety standards.

Are shipments compliant with GCC municipality regulations & CEPA?

Yes. Every batch complies with ESMA, SFDA, and local municipality food safety standards (including Dubai FoodWatch / ZAD registration). We supply Chamber of Commerce Certificates of Origin allowing zero or preferential customs duty under the India-UAE CEPA agreement.

Do you provide third-party laboratory test reports?

Yes. Upon request, batch-level inspection reports from ISO 17025 / NABL-accredited labs (Eurofins, SGS, or Bureau Veritas) are provided covering pesticide residue limits (MRL), aflatoxin screening, purity, and moisture levels.

Can we request customized sorting, grading, or private labeling?

Yes. We offer millimeter sizing calibration for vegetables, custom broken-grain percentages for rice, optical color sorting for spices/pulses, and bilingual English/Arabic private label bag printing.

03

Financials & Trade Terms

What are your standard export payment terms?

Our standard terms are 30% T/T deposit on order confirmation with 70% balance paid against digital shipping document copies (B/L, COO, Phytosanitary). Confirmed, irrevocable Letters of Credit (L/C) at sight are accepted for established corporate buyers.

Which Incoterms do you trade under?

We primarily quote CIF and CFR to major GCC destination ports. FOB Nhava Sheva (JNPT) or Mundra Port is also available upon request.

Claims & Dispute Resolution

Formal procedure for reporting and settling quality variances on bulk shipments to GCC ports.

01

24-Hour Notice Window

Report visually evident defects or temperature breaches within 24 hours of container discharge.

02

Independent Survey

Claims require an official inspection report from SGS or Bureau Veritas at the port of discharge.

03

3% Tolerance Threshold

Claims processed only if verified defect rate exceeds 3% total batch weight.

04

7-Day Settlement

Validated claims settled via price adjustments, replacement volumes, or credit notes within 7 business days.
