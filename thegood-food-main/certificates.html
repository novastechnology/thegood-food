Government & Global Bodies

Certifications & Registrations

All exports are supported by statutory registrations under official export development authorities of India and international food safety bodies.

APEDA

APEDA Registration (RCMC)

Registration-cum-Membership Certificate under the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (Ministry of Commerce & Industry, Govt. of India). Authorizes international trade and traceability for fresh produce, rice, and agricultural food products.

SPICE

Spices Board of India (CRES)

Certificate of Registration as Exporter of Spices issued by the Spices Board (Ministry of Commerce & Industry, Govt. of India). Validates mandatory quality compliance, aflatoxin and pesticide residue clearance, and authorization to export authentic Indian whole and ground spices globally.

FS

FSSAI Central License

Food Safety and Standards Authority of India central license authorizing food processing, bulk storage, and international export operations.

PHY

Phytosanitary Certification

National Plant Protection Organization (NPPO) clearance verifying all vegetable, rice, pulse, and spice consignments are pest-free, disease-free, and methyl bromide fumigated prior to vessel departure.

CO

Certificate of Origin (COO)

Issued by authorized Chambers of Commerce under India-UAE CEPA and GCC trade agreements for preferential tariff entry into UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Bahrain, and Kuwait.

ISO

ISO 22000 & ISO 9001

Internationally audited Food Safety Management and Quality Management Systems ensuring end-to-end traceability from farm procurement to container seal.

HA

HACCP Compliance

Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points protocol implemented across packhouse sorting, grading, and reefer stuffing lines.

GAP

Global G.A.P. Alignment

Good Agricultural Practices adherence across our grower partnerships ensuring sustainable farming, zero prohibited chemical residues, and worker safety.
