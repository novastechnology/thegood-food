Export Terms

Minimum Order & Logistics Specifications

FCL container parameters tailored for wholesale distributors, hypermarkets, and institutional food processors.

01

Container Standards (MOQ)

Fresh Produce: 40ft High-Cube Reefer Containers (~20–29 MT per crop).
Rice, Pulses & Spices: 20ft Normal Dry FCL Containers (~24–26 MT for rice/pulses, ~17–20 MT for spices).

02

Origin & Packing Centers

Onions & Potatoes: Maharashtra & Gujarat · Garlic: Madhya Pradesh · Tomatoes: Karnataka · Rice: Punjab & Haryana · Spices: Rajasthan, Gujarat & Andhra Pradesh.

03

Payment Terms

30% T/T deposit on order confirmation; 70% against shipping document copies. Irrevocable, confirmed L/C at sight accepted for established wholesale buyers.

04

Lead Time to Port

7–14 days from contract signing to container loading and vessel departure at Nhava Sheva (JNPT) or Mundra Port.

05

Incoterms & Discharge

CIF and CFR to major Middle East ports (Jebel Ali, Jeddah, Dammam, Shuwaikh, Hamad, Sohar). FOB Nhava Sheva / Mundra available on request.

06

Pre-Shipment Quality Inspections

Third-party testing & batch inspection certificates (SGS / Eurofins / Bureau Veritas) supplied for MRL, aflatoxin, moisture, and grain length verification.

Custom Sorting & Grading

We adjust optical sorting grids to meet your exact dimensional and grading specifications prior to export.
