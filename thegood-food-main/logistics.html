Quality Assurance

Zero-Defect Bulk Supply Protocol

Multi-stage QA from field harvest and milling to port discharge — compliant with ESMA and GCC municipality standards.

01

Origin Inspection & Crop Selection

Agronomists and quality inspectors test moisture, purity, sizing, and defect rates at source.

02

Triple-Tier Grading & Cleaning

Optical color sorting, destoning, and manual grading remove all foreign matter and defective units.

03

MRL & Phytosanitary Screening

NABL-accredited laboratory screening verifies pesticide residues, aflatoxins, and heavy metals below statutory GCC limits.

04

Pre-Loading Container Verification

Pre-cooling checks for reefers and moisture barrier liners for dry containers before customs seal.

05

Joint Discharge Survey

Independent surveyors verify seal numbers, pulp temperatures, and container integrity at GCC discharge ports.
