Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for wholesale importers sourcing bulk produce from India to the GCC.

01

Logistics & Delivery

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard MOQ is one full 40 - feet refrigerated container (reefer) & full 20 - feet dry container per commodity. We do not offer less-than-container load (LCL) shipping for fresh produce to maintain strict cold chain integrity.

Which GCC ports do you service?

We regularly discharge at Jebel Ali Port (Dubai), Khalifa Port (Abu Dhabi), Port Khalid (Sharjah), Dammam/Jeddah (KSA), Shuwaikh (Kuwait), Hamad (Qatar), and Salalah (Oman).

How do you prevent spoilage during transit?

We utilize advanced controlled atmosphere (CA) reefers equipped with continuous satellite telematics. Temperature, humidity, and oxygen levels are actively managed 24/7 based on each crop’s requirements.

What are typical shipping lead times from India?

Ocean freight from Indian Ocean hubs takes 5 to 7 days to GCC ports. Exact schedules are confirmed at the time of order booking.

02

Quality & Compliance

Are shipments compliant with GCC import regulations?

Yes. Every batch complies with ESMA regulations, MOCCAE standards, and local Dubai/Abu Dhabi municipality rules. All packaging includes bilingual English and Arabic labeling.

Do you provide Phytosanitary Certificates?

Absolutely. All shipments include a Government-issued Phytosanitary Certificate, Certificate of Origin, Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and SGS/Bureau Veritas pre-loading inspection reports on request.

Can we request custom sorting, sizing, or grading?

Yes. We adjust optical sorting grids to meet your exact dimensional and grading specifications (e.g., specific millimeter calibrations for potatoes or onions) prior to export.

03

Financials & Contracts

What are your standard payment terms?

Our standard framework requires a 30% telegraphic transfer (T/T) deposit upon order confirmation, with the remaining 70% balanced against digital copies of shipping documents. Irrevocable Letters of Credit (L/C) at sight are accepted for verified tier-one distributors.

How do you handle currency fluctuations?

All primary wholesale contracts and invoices are denominated in United States Dollars (USD) or UAE Dirhams (AED) to ensure long-term price stability for Gulf buyers.

Claims & Dispute Resolution

Formal procedure for reporting and settling quality variances on bulk shipments to GCC ports.

01

24-Hour Notice Window

Report visually evident defects or temperature breaches within 24 hours of container discharge.

02

Independent Survey

Claims require an official inspection report from SGS or Bureau Veritas at the port of discharge.

03

3% Tolerance Threshold

Claims processed only if verified defect rate exceeds 3% total batch weight.

04

7-Day Settlement

Validated claims settled via price adjustments, replacement volumes, or credit notes within 7 business days.
