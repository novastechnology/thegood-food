Why Choose Us

Unbroken cold chain. Guaranteed volume. Seamless port logistics.

Shipping fresh produce across the Arabian Sea requires absolute precision. We eliminate the risks of rot, weight loss, and customs delays.

01

Advanced Post-Harvest Pre-Cooling

Within hours of harvest, produce enters forced-air or vacuum pre-cooling chambers - halting ripening and locking in moisture before container sealing.

02

Smart Reefer & Microclimate Control

20ft and 40ft reefers with automated logging. Onions at 5-12Â°C, potatoes at 4-7°C, tomatoes at 10-12°C with continuous dehumidification.

03

High-Volume Packing & Palletization

Premium Lennow mesh bags, ventilated jute, and reinforced crates. All cargo palletized, strapped, and shrink-wrapped for rough sea conditions.

04

Strategic Port Proximity

Facilities near Nhava Sheva (JNPT), Mumbai and Mundra Port, Gujarat. Direct tier-one shipping lines cut Gulf transit to 3-7 days.
